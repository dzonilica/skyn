Writing tips
  • Put *stars* around words to make them italic, like the headings on the site: Before & *after.*
  • A link looks like this: [Vagaro booking page](https://www.vagaro.com/theskynroom1/services)
  • Press Enter for a new line.
  • Empty a box to hide that text. Use original brings back the text the website launched with.

How to use your studio

Everything you save here shows on theskynroom.com right away. Refresh the website to see it.

Treatment menu

  1. Add a treatment: tap + Treatment, fill in the name, category, price and time the same way they appear on Vagaro, then tap Save.
  2. Change a treatment: tap it in the list, edit, and save.
  3. Change the order: use the ↑ ↓ arrows. The website follows the same order.
  4. Hide without deleting: open the treatment and switch off Show on website. Useful for seasonal or paused services.
  5. Categories are the headings on the menu, like Facials or Add-ons. Tap + Category to add one. The arrows next to a category move the whole group. Tap a category name to rename, hide or delete it.
  6. Photo cards: a treatment with a photo and Show as a photo card switched on also appears as a large card on the Treatments page and in the slider on the Home page.
  7. Booking link: leave it empty and the Book button opens your Vagaro menu. To send clients straight to one service, open that service on Vagaro, copy the address from the browser and paste it in.

Shop

  1. Add a product: tap + Product, then add the name, brand, price, a photo and a description. The Shop page shows the first sentence; clients tap View More to read the whole description on the product's own page.
  2. Buy link: if the product can be bought online (for example a Vagaro, Stripe or Amazon link), paste the link and the website shows a Buy Now button. Leave it empty and the product shows Available in the studio.
  3. Sold out: switch it on to keep the product on the page, marked as sold out.
  4. Category is optional, for example Cleansers, Serums or SPF. Once two or more are used, the Shop page shows filter buttons.

Before & after (Results tab)

  1. Add a photo: tap + Photo, choose the before & after picture and give it a short title, then tap Save.
  2. Order: use the ↑ ↓ arrows. The first six photos open the Results page as a spread; the rest follow below them.
  3. Home page: switch on Show on the Home page for up to three photos. Their title (and treatment, if you add one) shows under each photo.

Text and photos on the pages (Pages tab)

  1. Pick a page at the top: Home, Treatments, Shop and so on. Footer is the bottom part that every page shares.
  2. Change the words in any box. Boxes are in the same order as on the page, grouped by section.
  3. Change a photo: tap Change photo under it.
  4. Save: the bar at the bottom shows how many changes you have; tap Save to put them on the website, or Undo to drop them.
  5. Made a mistake? Use original brings back the text or photo the website launched with.

Photos

Any photo from your phone or computer works; it is resized for the website automatically. Portrait photos (taller than wide) fit the cards best, and product photos look best on a plain background.

Your secret link

This link is the only key to your studio, so keep it private. Anyone who has it can edit your website. Bookmark it or add it to your phone's home screen.

If you think someone else has your link, make a new one. The old link stops working straight away on every device.
