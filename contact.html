Facial booking FAQs.

Which facial should I book as a new client?+

Book the Signature Custom Facial for your first appointment. Melissa will assess your skin, review your routine and discuss your concerns before recommending future treatments.

How do I book an appointment?+

Open the Vagaro booking page, select your treatment and choose an available appointment time.

What happens at my first visit?+

Your Signature Custom Facial starts with a consultation about your skin history, skincare routine and goals. Melissa then tailors the facial to your skin’s needs.

When will I see results?+

Results vary by skin concern and treatment. During your consultation, Melissa will explain what to expect and discuss a treatment plan based on your goals.

Where can I see before and after photos?+

View client before and after photos, or visit @skynbymelissa on Instagram for more treatment updates.
