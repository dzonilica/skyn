Meet Melissa.

I’m Melissa Wohl, founder and esthetician at The Skyn Room. I provide facial treatments tailored to your skin concerns and goals.

(My story)

My interest in skincare began long before I became an esthetician. After exploring other career paths, I chose to turn that interest into my work.

My own skin concerns taught me how frustrating it can be to search for answers. I understand the patience it takes to find a routine that works and see changes in your skin.

I founded The Skyn Room to offer care that starts with understanding your skin. Before choosing a treatment, I review your skin concerns, history, current routine and goals. We use that conversation to plan your facial and next steps.

How I plan your facial.

Your treatment plan starts with a skin assessment. I consider your concerns, sensitivity and current routine when choosing your facial.

Consultation first

We discuss your skin concerns, products and daily routine. This helps me understand what you want to address before we start your facial.

Care tailored to your skin

I choose each step of your facial based on your skin assessment and goals, with attention to how your skin responds.

Honest expectations

We discuss what your treatment can address and what may need ongoing care. Your progress depends on your skin, treatment plan and consistency.

Time to relax

Your appointment combines focused skincare with time to relax. You can ask questions about your facial and the next steps in your care.
